# Third-party sources

This repository includes or derives information from external scientific resources. Upstream material retains its original terms; consult the source provider before redistribution or reuse outside this research package.

Principal upstream resources include:

- NCBI/GenBank reference and conservation sequences; accessions and retrieval provenance are recorded under `data/` and the manuscript.
- 1000 Genomes HLA typing data used for the genotype-based coverage analysis; source citation and manifest are under `data/population/`.
- IEDB next-generation tool outputs and population-coverage results; retrieval metadata are recorded with the archived outputs.
- Predictor-derived B-cell tables and archived structure-prediction metrics used as frozen external-tool outputs.

The repository's Apache-2.0 and CC BY 4.0 grants apply only to material for which Summus Stuprator has the right to grant those permissions.
